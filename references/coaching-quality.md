# Keep the judgment and the lesson connected

Use this shared check when writing or rendering a critique. It strengthens the existing rubric; it is not a new scoring scale or evidence that photographic quality has been validated.

## A whole-photo conclusion, not a repair list

In two or three sentences, say what makes this particular photograph work, the most consequential limitation or uncertainty, and whether correction is worthwhile. A sound image may need preservation rather than repair. Do not headline a small cut, a preferred palette or a conventional rule at the expense of the relationships that make the frame worthwhile. Do not invent a story or an intention to supply meaning.

Keep visible evidence, viewing effect, purpose conditions and preference distinct where they affect the recommendation. A direction such as “earlier shutter” is a proposed experiment, not proof that the unseen alternative would be better. Name the successful relationships the experiment must preserve.

## Connect the next exercise

The leading suggestion names a particular observation. Practice normally changes one variable related to that same observation and gives a visible success criterion. If practising a successful relationship instead, say explicitly that this is consolidation, name the protected observation and explain why it is the useful next step. Merely repeating the same subject or genre is not alignment.

New visual reports include `coaching`: `action_type`, zero-based integer `observation_index`, `exercise_observation_index`, `exercise_mode` (`围绕重点` or `巩固优点`), `exercise_connection`, and `success_criterion`. Use `优先修正` for a structural bottleneck, `按目的选择` for a genuine purpose branch, and `局部修正`, `可选尝试` or `保留` when no reconstruction is required. The observation referenced by the priority is `首要`, or an explicit `要保护` for preservation. Consolidation refers to `要保护`; otherwise the exercise and priority indices match. These links identify the target, not proof that the prose describes it correctly.

## Make scores interpretable

Retain the eight original dimensions and 0–5 integer intervals; do not add a total or rescore a previous photograph to make a revised interface look better. Explain the localized evidence supporting each interval and a real limit on a higher assessment when one exists. A strong dimension need not receive a manufactured fault. Unknown personal relationships or unstated intentions are not photographic defects.

New API scores carry `confidence` (`高`, `中`, `低`) and nullable `uncertainty`. This is the model's description of its evidential footing, not calibrated confidence or a measured probability. Medium/low footing names a specific limitation. For unavailable evidence, do not turn uncertainty into zero, and do not treat `N/A` as “bad.” Older reports without these fields must not acquire invented confidence labels.

## A check can be unresolved

Integrity verdicts also allow `无法判断`. Use it when the relevant pixels, original resolution or reliable geometry/material reference are insufficient. `观察` means a visible watch item; `通过` requires an actual inspected basis. For mixed cases, report the visible part and state which part remains unresolved. A 1600-pixel preview cannot certify fine focus, subtle artifacts or original-file recoverability.

## Before delivery

Reconcile the conclusion, decision, leading suggestion, score reasons, proposed edit and practice target. A “no rebuild” decision can coexist with a small correction, but not a demand that the whole frame must be rebuilt. Check the source and any actual treatment at comparable framing: name what improved, what worsened and what remains uncertain. Producing a file or changing pixels does not prove improvement; if the treatment has not been inspected, label it as unreviewed.

The local validator checks links, decision/action compatibility and a narrow set of explicit reconstruction contradictions. It cannot understand every sentence, authenticate a visible claim or prove an aesthetic improvement. Do not add silent paid retries or claim an independent review from one model request. Real-time references, true before/after review and independent photographic evaluation require their own implementation and verification.
